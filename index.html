Engineer at the meeting point of hardware, software, and AI

I started out in the physical world — a Bachelor's in Maritime Electrical Automation, certified electrician credentials, and years of hands-on offshore commissioning. That background taught me how real systems fail and what it takes to make them reliable when the stakes are high.

From there I moved up the stack — into the software that runs underwater production equipment, offshore platforms, and critical infrastructure. Today my focus is full-stack software and AI engineering: I design, build, and operate LLM-driven systems and web applications, and I use AI tooling as a core part of how I ship.

The combination — industrial discipline paired with modern, AI-augmented software engineering — is what I bring to a team.
